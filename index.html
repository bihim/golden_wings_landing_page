Why Electric

Built For Bangladesh's Roads

Heat, humidity, monsoon traffic, unpredictable fuel prices — VOSENBON is engineered around the realities of daily riding here.

Fast Home Charging

Plug into any standard socket at home — no dedicated charging station or extra wiring required.

Low Running Cost

A full charge costs a fraction of a litre of petrol. Ride further for less, every single day.

Smart Anti-Theft

Motion-triggered alarm and remote immobiliser keep your VOSENBON where you parked it.

All-Weather Build

Sealed electricals and rust-resistant frame rated for monsoon rides and summer heat alike.
